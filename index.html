DIAGNOSIS

How I approach a slow Android screen

1. ReproduceDefine the exact user-visible symptom.
2. MeasureFrame timing, traces, CPU, allocations, network and database behavior.
3. IsolateComposition, layout/draw, I/O, image work, main-thread blocking or repeated work.
4. FixApply the smallest high-impact remediation.
5. VerifyCompare before/after evidence and guard against regressions.
